## Overview

At **Patricia Technologies**, a premier Nigerian cryptocurrency and digital asset exchange, my role was centered on aggressive infrastructure optimization and scaling. Dealing with highly volatile digital assets required backend systems that were not just fast, but mathematically secure and completely immune to race conditions.

## Key Architectural Wins

### Throughput & Scaling

The primary challenge at Patricia was dealing with explosive user growth. I systematically identified memory leaks and un-indexed queries within the core infrastructure, optimizing the backend throughput to handle 3x the standard load capacity during volatile crypto market peaks.

### Legacy Elimination

I spearheaded the refactoring of legacy codebases, transforming monolithic, tightly coupled logic into modular and highly maintainable service classes. This directly decreased our incident mean-time-to-resolution (MTTR) and allowed the engineering team to ship features iteratively with extreme confidence.

## Strategic Impact

- **Operational Efficiency:** Architected and contributed heavily to a comprehensive internal admin dashboard. This allowed the operations team to securely manage crypto liquidities, view automated risk flags, and process user inquiries without relying on developer intervention.

## Technologies Used

- **Languages / Frameworks:** NodeJS, ExpressJS, TypeScript, PHP
- **Databases:** PostgreSQL, Redis
- **DevOps:** AWS, Docker
