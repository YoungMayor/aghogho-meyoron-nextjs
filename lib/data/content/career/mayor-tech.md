## Overview

At **Mayor Tech Solutions**, I transitioned deeply into leadership and end-to-end organizational architecture. Tasked with transforming raw in-house ideas into fully-fledged web and mobile applications, I built not only the software—but the team that powered it.

## Key Wins & Impact

### Team Assembly & Mentorship

I recruited and led a specialized team of 5 developers. My primary focus was establishing a rigorous coding culture, enforcing tight version control, and accelerating the onboarding process so that each member could contribute to live production environments immediately.

### In-House Product Suite

I single-handedly engineered the foundational in-house product suite. From the company's internal operational dashboard and proprietary developer community platform down to blog architecture, automated video scraping sites, and expansive church/event management tools.

### Agency & Contracting

Beyond internal products, I pushed the team outward to function as an agency. We partnered flawlessly on over 17 distinct web-based contract projects, reliably delivering high-quality monolithic Laravel and PHP applications on incredibly tight deadlines.

## Technologies Used

- **Languages / Frameworks:** PHP, Laravel, JavaScript, Vue.js, Bootstrap
- **Databases:** MySQL, Redis
- **DevOps:** Shared Hosting, cPanel, WebHooks, Git
