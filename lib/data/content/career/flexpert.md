## Overview

My journey at **Flexpert (Flyte iSystems)**, an IT solutions provider based in Benin City, served as the bedrock of my engineering career. Starting as an intern, I rapidly evolved into a prolific backend contributor, engaging directly in the creation of over 5 custom client applications and several in-house administrative platforms.

## Key Responsibilities & Growth

### Core PHP & Client Deliverables

During this two-year period, I abandoned the comfort of pre-built solutions and dove deep into Core PHP and raw MySQL. I built numerous dynamic web platforms—from blogs and job agencies to complete e-commerce and social media platforms using Ajax and seamless DOM manipulation long before modern SPA frameworks became the norm.

### API & Gateway Integration

I learned the vital necessity of third-party integration, writing and maintaining highly reliable interfaces linking our clients to payment gateways, job boards, and real-time news syndication platforms.

### Team Collaboration & Scalability

I co-developed specialized internal tools, including the Staff Management Console, Academy Student Tracking Panel, and Task Allocation Dashboards. Earning the "Grand Team Player" certificate, I demonstrated early on my ability to harmonize with 14 other developers and bridge the gap between web development and mobile app integration.

## Technologies Used

- **Languages:** PHP (Core/Vanilla), JavaScript (Ajax, DOM), MySQL
- **Integration:** Payment Gateways (Stripe/Paystack variants), RESTful data feeds
