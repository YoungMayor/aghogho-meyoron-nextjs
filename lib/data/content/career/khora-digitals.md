## Overview

In my specialized contract with **Khora Digitals**, I was tasked with the rigorous optimization of their in-house framework and the rapid delivery of client-facing web solutions. This role demanded extreme adaptability, diving into an unfamiliar, proprietary codebase and instantly producing tangible results.

## Key Wins & Impact

- **Rapid Client Delivery:** Spearheaded the development of two complete web solutions for client companies within a compressed three-month window. This required not just coding, but rapid prototyping and tight feedback loops with stakeholders.
- **In-House Optimization:** Functioned as a core contributor remotely, debugging and heavily optimizing the company's proprietary framework alongside two staff developers, vastly improving baseline render speeds and code security.

## Philosophy

Working with a proprietary framework requires an intrinsic understanding of design patterns over language syntax. I focused heavily on decoupling logic from the view layer and abstracting redundant queries, ensuring the internal toolset was robust enough for the team to use effortlessly going forward.

## Technologies Used

- **Stack:** PHP, Proprietary In-House MVC Framework, MySQL
- **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, jQuery
