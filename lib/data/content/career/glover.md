## Overview

During my tenure at **Glover Techstars**, I functioned as a core architectural pillar for a high-traffic digital marketplace transacting millions in gift cards, airtime, and digital assets. My purview extended far beyond standard backend development—I was systematically tasked with solving severe bottlenecks, engineering high-integrity financial mechanics, and guaranteeing zero-downtime scalability across our entire service mesh.

## Key Architectural Wins

### Zero-Downtime Infrastructure Upgrade

I led and architected a monumental system migration from **Laravel v7 to v12**. This was executed with zero downtime, ensuring continuous service for one of Nigeria's leading fintech aggregators. This upgrade vastly improved the application's runtime efficiency, enabled modern PHP features, and drastically reduced technical debt across the codebase.

### Fraud Mitigation & OCR Engine

Gift card fraud represents a critical vector for financial loss. To combat this, I engineered a proprietary **OCR-powered validation engine** wrapped in AES-256 encryption. This system automatically scanned, verified, and safely vaulted digital assets, dropping our manual reviews and mitigating high-risk transaction fraud entirely.

### High-Concurrency Rewards Engine

I designed a custom gamification and rewards engine from the ground up. By utilizing **Redis** and highly optimized relational data structures, I ensured the system could handle tens of thousands of concurrent requests during peak marketing promos (e.g., Spin-the-wheel, Referral payouts) without ever locking the database or delaying primary financial transactions.

## Strategic Impact

- **Customer Resolution Acceleration:** Integrated the **Freshdesk API** into our core administrative dashboard, unifying support operations and directly improving customer resolution speed and retention.
- **In-House Reward Currency:** Instrumental in mathematically defining and coding an internal reward currency system securely tied to user engagement milestones.
- **Cross-Border Identity Verification:** Shipped cross-country KYC workflows, seamlessly onboarding international users while adhering strictly to regional compliance standards.

## Technologies Used

- **Languages / Frameworks:** PHP 8+, Laravel 12, TypeScript
- **Databases & Caching:** PostgreSQL, Redis, MariaDB
- **Security & Tools:** AES-256 Encryption, OCR Validation Pipelines, Docker, GitHub Actions
